Open-source software for cognitive neuroscience and narrative research.

narRaters

narRaters (narrative + raters) turns story audio or text and participant recalls into structured, reviewable data — with a web UI at every step. Open source on GitHub: github.com/xianNeuro/narRaters.

Transcribe audio, segment stories into events, correct and parse recalls, match recalls to story events, and rate causal links — with human review before export.
