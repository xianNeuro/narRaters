narRaters
---------
narRaters (narrative + raters) turns story audio or text and participant recalls into structured, reviewable data — with a web UI at every step. Open source on GitHub: github.com/xianNeuro/narRaters

Pipeline steps: transcribe audio · segment story events · correct recalls · parse clauses · match recalls to events · rate causal links — with human review before export.
